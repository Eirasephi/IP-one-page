1. About the IP Benchbook Series

The WIPO Intellectual Property Benchbook Series is a collection of practical manuals designed to support judges, legal practitioners, and policymakers in understanding and adjudicating IP disputes. According to WIPO, the series provides guidance on IP law and procedure, offering insights into judicial management of IP cases across different jurisdictions.

Purpose

  • Strengthen judicial capacity in IP adjudication
  • Provide structured guidance on handling IP disputes
  • Share good practices from experienced judges
  • Support consistent and informed decision-making in IP cases

Key Features

  • Country-specific manuals
  • Focus on procedural and substantive aspects of IP law
  • Written by judges and IP experts
  • Part of WIPO’s broader effort to support national IP ecosystems

2. National Model for Institutional IP Policies

WIPO’s National Model for Institutional IP Policies provides a framework to help universities and research institutions manage intellectual property effectively. It supports institutions in defining ownership, disclosure, commercialization, and benefit-s haring practices.

Purpose

  • Help institutions manage IP generated through research
  • Encourage innovation and technology transfer
  • Clarify roles, responsibilities, and ownership
  • Reduce legal and reputational risks in partnerships

Core Components

  • IP ownership and rights of use
  • Disclosure and management of inventions
  • Incentives and benefits-haring for researchers
  • Conflict of interest management
  • Alignment with national innovation strategies
